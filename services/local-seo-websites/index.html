Technical SEO built into the website

Quartz can build search-friendly websites with crawlable HTML, canonical URLs, robots directives, structured data and automatically maintained sitemaps where the platform supports it.

Local relevance comes from real information

Strong local pages use genuine business information such as services, address, city, area, opening hours, phone, images and useful descriptions. Repeating city names or keyword lists is not a substitute for useful content.

Indexing support without false guarantees

A technically correct site can make discovery easier, but Google decides when and whether a page is indexed and how it ranks. Quartz focuses on the parts that can be controlled: quality, structure, performance and crawlability.
